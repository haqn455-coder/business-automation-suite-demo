# GroupOps Owner Control Center

Synthetic prospect demo shell for the Business Automation Suite.

## Locked prospect architecture
- Group owner view with three primary divisions: Petrol Pumps, LED, Solar.
- Fuel reuses the frozen FuelOps V1 and adds a representative source-level station cockpit in this shell.
- LED is import/wholesale-first with connected local project/site operations.
- Solar is import/wholesale-first with connected project/installation/owned-site O&M.
- Shared owner controls: exceptions, exposure, approvals, evidence, accountability and audit.

No real customer data, bank credentials, vendor credentials, paid AI/API calls or autonomous financial actions are used.
